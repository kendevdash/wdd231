NP Membership Benefits

  • Listing in the online business directory
  • Access to chamber networking events
  • Monthly newsletter subscription
  • No membership fee for qualifying non-profits

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Business listing with logo in the directory
  • One free event ticket per quarter
  • Discounted rates on future advertising

Silver Membership Benefits

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Featured placement in the member directory
  • Quarterly spotlight eligibility on the home page
  • Two free event tickets per quarter
  • 10% discount on chamber-hosted training workshops

Gold Membership Benefits

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Priority spotlight eligibility on the home page
  • Unlimited event tickets
  • Free booth at the annual Chamber Expo
  • 25% discount on chamber-hosted training workshops
  • Logo placement on the chamber home page
